# Data notice

- Files in `data/` and `outputs/`, and the maps in `docs/`, contain data derived from
  OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
  License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Population figures: IBGE Census 2022 (Brazil) and US Census Bureau American
  Community Survey 5-year estimates (USA). Public domain or open government data.
- Elevation: Open-Meteo elevation API, based on the Copernicus DEM.
- `data/marica/stations.csv`: existing station positions digitised from a study map
  (about ±110 m). Verify against the operator's records before operational use.
- No internal or confidential operator data is included in this repository.
