Open-source bike-share planning

Where should a city put its next bike-share stations?

A hexagon-grid method that scores neighbourhoods on population, services, transit, cycling infrastructure, coverage gaps and terrain, then proposes new stations in phases using siting rules from operating Bublr Bikes in Milwaukee.

Coverage or transit? Lessons from Milwaukee

How Bublr balances station coverage against bus connections, with an interactive map of the city scored on six criteria.

Maricá bike-share expansion plan

The same rules applied to Maricá, Brazil: 52 proposed stations in three phases, raising coverage within 500 m from about 34% to 63% of residents.
